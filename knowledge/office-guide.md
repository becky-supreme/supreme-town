# Supreme Town guide

- **Moving:** click the floor to walk there, or use the arrow keys / WASD. Scroll to zoom in and out.
- **Talking:** your microphone and camera connect automatically to whoever can hear you. At the desks that is only someone right next to you. In the streets and kitchen it is anyone within about four squares. In a shared space (the co-working lounge, The Afterworks, the lunch area, a team social corner, the meeting room) everyone in that space hears each other and nobody outside does. The label in the top-left corner tells you when everyone can hear you.
- **Arcade:** two cabinets in The Afterworks. Press E to play Lead Volley; you play the computer until someone else joins the same cabinet, then it is 1v1, first to 5.
- **Paid & Organic:** the team for paid ads, paid social and organic is at the far end of the back wall, past the meeting room. Its receptionist is Juno.
- **Screen sharing:** click Share screen in the bottom bar. Everyone you are currently talking to sees it; they can click it to make it bigger. Click Stop sharing when done.
- **Status:** the switch at the bottom sets you to Available, In a meeting, or Busy (do not disturb). In a meeting and Busy both turn off your mic and camera and silence the town for you, without you leaving. Your status shows on a sign over whichever desk you are sitting at.
- **Desks:** every desk is open. Click a desk to sit at it. Press E at a desk to change how it looks: there are six designs, or you can describe your own, and anyone can change any desk at any time.
- **Sitting:** walk onto any chair, armchair, bean bag or stool and you sit down.
- **Team areas:** Dev & Supreme Intelligence, Content and Design are along the back wall. AM & Ads and Digital Strategy are across Main Street. Each has six desks, a receptionist and a small team social corner.
- **Team receptionists:** SI (Dev & Supreme Intelligence), Paige (Content), Pixel (Design), Amy (AM & Ads) and Dex (Digital Strategy). Ask them how their team works. A "Needs context" bubble means that team has not given its receptionist anything to read yet.
- **Co-working lounge:** the big room at the front left. Sofas, armchairs, bean bags and four long shared tables for working together.
- **The Afterworks bar:** front right, next to the lounge. Grab a beer, cocktail or soft drink at the bar (press E), play at the pool tables, sit on the bar stools. This is the drinks social.
- **Kitchen:** grab a coffee or lunch at the counters (press E) and carry it around.
- **Non-working social lunch:** the lunch tables next to the kitchen. No work talk.
- **Meeting room:** the glass room at the far end of the back wall. Everyone inside hears each other at full volume and nobody outside can hear in.
- **Rides:** go-karts, bikes, scooters and hoverboards are parked at the entrance and along the second street. Press E to hop on and X to hop off.
- **Dancing:** press V, or the Dance button, to dance. It works anywhere, and on the dance floor in The Afterworks there's music. Walking stops the dancing.
- **Sound:** footsteps, hopping on rides, grabbing drinks, the arcade and people walking in all make little sounds, and the dance floor plays music that gets louder as you get closer. The Sound button in the bottom bar turns it all off. It also goes quiet when you're In a meeting or Busy.
- **Decorating:** walk into a shared space (the lounge, the bar, the lunch area, the kitchen, the meeting room, the entrance or a team area) and press Decorate. Describe what you want, for example "it's Sam's birthday, balloons and a HAPPY BIRTHDAY SAM sign", and the AI dresses the whole area for everyone. It goes back to normal by itself after a day, or straight away with the "back to normal" button.
- **Waving, inviting and following:** click on another person to open a little menu. Wave makes your character wave and tells them you waved. Invite over sends them a note with a Go over button that walks them straight to you. Follow walks you after them wherever they go, until you click somewhere else, use the arrow keys or press Stop. People set to In a meeting or Busy aren't sent waves or invites, and you're told so.
- **Text chat:** type in the box at the bottom. Everyone in town sees it.
- **Characters:** when you come in you choose an outfit (including costumes), a hairstyle and colours.
